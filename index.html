Commissions and Pricing

Interested in personalized artwork? Explore my 2D and 3D commission options below (All prices in USD):

2D Commissions

Choose from sketches, line art, flat colors, or shading. Prices vary by character coverage (portrait, upper half, or full body), additional characters, and background complexity.

  • Sketch:
    • Portrait: $8
    • Upper Half: $10
    • Full Body: $12
  • Line Art:
    • Portrait: $12
    • Upper Half: $14
    • Full Body: $16
  • Flat Colors:
    • Portrait: $16
    • Upper Half: $18
    • Full Body: $20
  • Shading:
    • Portrait: $20
    • Upper Half: $22
    • Full Body: $24

Additional Costs:

Additional Characters: +50% of base price per extra character

Backgrounds: $20–$150 depending on complexity

NSFW Content: +20% base cost

3D Commissions

Offering fully rigged and textured 3D models, props, and character avatars. Prices vary based on complexity.

  • Low Poly Model: $150 – $225
  • Mid Poly Model: $225 – $300
  • High Poly Model: $300 – $500
  • Static Prop (from scratch): $30
  • Dynamic Prop (from scratch): $50
  • Kitbash Character: $75
  • Kitbash Prop: $75
  • Facial Visemes: $10 per viseme

Additional Costs:

NSFW Content: +20% base cost

VRChat Avatar Services

Fully customized VRChat avatars, rigging, optimization, and animation setups.

  • From Scratch Avatar: starting at $250
  • Kitbashing: starting at $100. Client-purchased bases and assets are extra.
  • Texturing: $30 – $100
  • Expressions: $10 each
  • Rigging: $30 – $100
  • Custom Clothing: $30 – $100 each
  • Dynamic Bones/Phys Bones Setup: $10 each
  • Toggle Setups (Animated Props & Gestures): $10 each
  • Custom Animations (Gestures, Emotes, Dances): $20 each
  • Quest Conversion (Optimized for Oculus): $50

Additional Costs:

Rush Order: +25% base cost

Commercial Use: +50% base cost

NSFW Content: +20% base cost

Extra Edits/Revisions: $10 each

Three complete revisions are included in the sketching phase. Extra edits and revisions beyond those rounds are $10 each.
