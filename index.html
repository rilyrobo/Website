FAQ

Are commissions open?

Yes. Use the request form on this page, or DM me on Discord or X.

How many revisions are included?

Three complete revisions in the sketching phase. Reasonable minor edits after completion, such as an eye-colour change, are included. Extra edits beyond those rounds are $10 each.

How do I pay?

PayPal, invoiced, in USD. Orders of $100 or less are paid up front. Orders above $100 are half up front and half on approval of the initial sketch. Currency conversion and payment-provider fees are the customer's responsibility.

How long does a commission take?

Usually 1–2 weeks; larger pieces may take longer — I'll keep you updated. You can ask for a refund if there has been no progress for two months, or if the work is not finished within four months. The Terms of Service lists the exceptions.

What won't you draw?

Extreme NSFW and extreme fetishes — if you have to ask, it's probably a no. I will draw blood and gore, costumes, poses, and light BDSM / rope. Accepted NSFW is +20% of the base cost.
